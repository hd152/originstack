What's new

The highlights of each release, in plain language. The full changelog has every change and the measurements behind them. To update, download the latest release; the app says in its header when a new one is out.

2.11 9–14 October 2026

2.11.9 14 Oct

  • Flatter backgrounds. Background extraction models every colour channel again; on a colour-calibrated stack it had quietly fallen back to a coarser method for green and blue. It is also twice as fast.
  • Faster re-finishing. --from-stack reuses its cached early steps even when you write to a new file name: 27 s down to 10 s per try on one stack.
  • Saved settings now keep skipped steps, and every preview follows the chosen stretch colour mode.

2.11.8 13 Oct

  • Re-stretch after a run. The app offers every stretch on the finished image; Apply to JPEG saves the one you like without re-processing.
  • Three new stretches: noise floor, OKLab and CLAHE. See the stretch gallery.
  • The preview stretch is per-channel by default again; --stretch-color preserve keeps colour ratios.

2.11.7 13 Oct

  • More stretches for faint nebulae: masked, multiscale and the SETI statistical stretch.
  • Gentler automatic local contrast on emission nebulae.

2.11.4–2.11.6 10–12 Oct

  • Big sessions on smaller machines. Frames are stored losslessly compressed when they would not fit in memory.
  • Large bright nebulae such as Orion show their faint outer parts and their core structure in the JPEG.
  • Already-debayered RGB FITS frames load again; the update notice shows reliably in the app.

2.11.0–2.11.3 9–10 Oct

  • Dutch for the app and the website.
  • A temp folder of your choice, checked for space before the run, and temporary files cleaned up after every run, even a cancelled or failed one.
  • Output can be a folder; the stack is named after the session and never overwrites an earlier one.
  • Very large sessions (850 frames) show progress instead of looking hung, and can be cancelled; closing the app always ends it cleanly.
  • Re-running a multi-night folder stacks the nights again instead of the previous result.

2.10 8–9 October 2026

2.10.2 9 Oct

  • Now under the GNU GPL v3. Still free to use, study, share and change; modified versions that are distributed must share their source too. Earlier releases remain under MIT.

2.10.0–2.10.1 8 Oct

  • Gaia answers from a local cache: re-runs and the next night of the same target colour-calibrate in milliseconds, and offline.
  • Colour calibration on hard fields: dense reddened nebulae and rich clusters whose bright stars are saturated now get a usable fit, and a fit that is too scattered is skipped rather than applied.
  • Seamless multi-night combines: no session edges, no red rims, white saturated stars.
  • --use-gpu was four times slower than the processor; now it matches it.

2.5–2.9 5–8 October 2026

2.9.0 8 Oct

  • Seven more languages for the app and the website: German, Spanish, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.
  • A measured profile of the Origin's camera, for honest photometry error bars and colour calibration on sparse fields.
  • Overexposed subs (twilight, moonlight) are rejected; comet sessions blend and resume reliably.

2.8.0 7 Oct

  • A much smaller download: the native module went from 26.6 MB to 2.7 MB with identical results.
  • This website.

2.7.0 7 Oct

  • A macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel.
  • Cleaner skies and crisper galaxy arms: local contrast no longer adds grain to the sky.
  • Multi-night folders pool into one deeper stack when the rotation allows; live stacking follows field rotation.
  • --full-field keeps the edges the normal stack crops away.

2.6.0 6 Oct

  • "What did you image?" picture cards and Full quality / Quick look in the app, with a summary of what Start will do, a description under every setting and a settings search.
  • Small faint targets render on a clean, dark sky; bright stars are no longer blue discs.

2.5.0 5 Oct

  • True star colours: Gaia colour calibration on by default.
  • Star reduction that narrows stars instead of blurring them.
  • Large galaxies no longer come out as a red slab when they cover much of the frame.

2.4 2 October 2026

2.4.0 2 Oct

  • Proper image coaddition, on by default: sharper stars at no cost in noise.
  • A built-in plate solver: about a second per solve, no account, offline once the area is cached.
  • --from-stack: re-run only the finishing steps on an earlier stack.
  • Photometry error bars from a measured noise model.

Older releases are in the changelog and on the releases page.
