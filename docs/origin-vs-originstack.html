What your Origin saved, and what OriginStack makes of it

Ten sessions from one Celestron Origin. In each picture, the left side is the stack the telescope saved itself, and the right side is OriginStack's result from the same session's frames, with one click and default settings. Drag across a picture to compare.

Ten sessions, the same frames

To keep the comparison fair, the Origin's image is shown with a standard one-click auto-stretch, because the file it saves is too flat to look at directly. How each side was made is described below.

How these were made

Left: the Origin

The left side is the FinalStackedMaster.tiff the Origin writes into each session folder. Its sky sits at mid-grey, so it is shown with PixInsight's standard automatic screen stretch at its published defaults: shadows clipped 2.8 noise units below the sky, sky placed at 25% brightness, each colour channel separately. These values were not tuned for this comparison. The Origin app may show its own image differently.

Right: OriginStack

The right side is OriginStack run on the same session folder with no options, and the preview JPEG it writes. Nothing was edited afterwards.

Matching the two

The Origin's image was rotated and shifted onto OriginStack's using a few hundred to a few thousand matched stars per target. The two stacks differ by about 180° plus up to 9° of field rotation. OriginStack trims the edges that not every frame covers, so the whole-frame view shows the area both images share.

What the difference is

Most of the difference is in what happens after stacking: background and colour calibration, noise reduction, star reduction and the stretch. On noise at the scale of a star, OriginStack's stack itself ties the Origin's own.

Timing

Run times are wall-clock for the whole run, from loading the frames to the finished image, on a 2020 laptop: Intel Core i9-10885H with 8 cores and 64 GB of RAM. The ten sessions took 1,178 seconds together, about two minutes each.

tools/bench_vs_origin.py measures OriginStack against the Origin's own stack on your own sessions.
